WordNet 3.1 Licence
===================

TypoZen ships dictionary.tsv and thesaurus.tsv, which are derived from the
Princeton University WordNet 3.1 database. They are generated by
tools/Make-Dictionary.ps1, which reads WordNet's data files and writes a flat
word/definition and word/synonyms mapping. No WordNet source files are shipped.
