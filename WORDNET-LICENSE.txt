Dictionary and Thesaurus Data Licence
=====================================

TypoZen ships dictionary.tsv and thesaurus.tsv, which are derived from the
Open English WordNet, 2025+ edition (the 2025 edition plus its curated
proper nouns). Open English WordNet is itself derived from Princeton
University's WordNet 3.1. Both must be credited, and both notices follow.

Changes made by TypoZen: tools/Make-Dictionary.ps1 reads the WordNet data
files (data.noun, data.verb, data.adj, data.adv) and writes them out as two
flat lists -- word -> definitions, and word -> synonyms -- keeping a limited
number of senses per word, most common first. How common a sense is comes
from the sense frequency counts (cntlist.rev) of Princeton WordNet 3.1, used
only to choose and order senses. No other content is added or altered, and no
Open English WordNet or WordNet source files are shipped.

Open English WordNet: https://en-word.net/
Source and releases:  https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet
Princeton WordNet:    https://wordnet.princeton.edu/
