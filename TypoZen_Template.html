TypoZen

Paginated ePub reader and WYSIWYG Markdown editor — with two-page spreads and media support.

Writing

  • Dual-mode editing: Seamlessly switch between WYSIWYG Preview and raw Source without losing your scroll position.
  • Live block editing for Markdown and text: headings, lists, tables, tasks, emphasis, fenced code.
  • Deep immersion: Engage Focus mode, Typewriter scroll, and chrome auto-hide to eliminate distractions while you work.
  • Engineered for massive files: A virtualized rendering engine keeps 40,000+ block documents fast and responsive.

Reading & Research

  • A first-class .epub reader: Paginated layout with a true two-page spread mode. Supports the publisher's native HTML, full TOC, reading scrubber, and per-tab session memory.
  • Built-in dictionary & thesaurus: Over 150,000 offline definitions. Select any word for instant definitions, synonyms, and document occurrence counts.
  • Document Search: Dedicated search sidebar (Alt+S) with full match highlighting and navigation. Acts as a seamless reader for ZenSeek searches.
  • Marks & Annotations: Highlight text, write notes, and drop bookmarks that intelligently survive document edits.
  • PDF, HTML, images, and media open read-only. Never dirty, never saved over.

Look, Session & Privacy

  • Bundled premium typography (Inter, Literata, Merriweather, Source Sans) ensures perfect rendering without network requests.
  • 25 curated built-in themes (dark, light, mono) plus Customise Theme… to build and save your own palettes.
  • Complete session restore: Remembers your window layout, theme, tabs, margins, and exact reading positions.
  • 100% Offline & Portable: Zero telemetry. Privacy Mode stops writing document history, positions, and recent files.

Files

  • Open Markdown, text, epub, PDF, common images and media. Save text as UTF-8 (atomic write). Export HTML; Print / PDF.
  • Links — hover for Open Link, Show in Folder and Edit Link. Local files open in a tab, http links in your browser, and #heading jumps within the document.
